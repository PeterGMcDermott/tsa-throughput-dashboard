What changed this week

Seven new days moved September from a partial read to a complete month and added the first three days of October. The broader national picture barely moved, but the airport-level leaders shifted.

  • September stabilized.Throughput reached 69.76M, just 10,382 passengers below September 2025. That follows declines of 1.3% in July and 2.7% in August.
  • The 12-month trend held.878.6M passengers were screened from Oct 2025 through Sep 2026, up 0.26% from the prior 12 months and effectively unchanged from last week's +0.3% read.
  • SFO moved into the top three growers.Its trailing-year gain reached +0.77M, behind ORD (+1.90M) and LGA (+1.43M), and ahead of AUS (+0.37M).
  • LAS became the largest absolute decliner.Its trailing-year loss reached −1.34M, ahead of LAX (−1.18M) and JFK (−0.97M).
  • Early October is encouraging, not conclusive.Oct 1–3 ran +0.74% year over year. Three days are too short to call a renewed growth trend.

Data notes

Methodology

Source & extraction

TSA FOIA Reading Room weekly passenger throughput PDFs (157 reports), parsed from vector layout.

Aggregation & coverage

Data runs October 1, 2023 through October 3, 2026: 36 complete calendar months through September 2026, plus October 2026 partial (3 of 31 days). The partial month is excluded from YoY comparisons and top-movers rankings.

Seasonality

TTM compares October 2025–September 2026 with October 2024–September 2025. Matching periods control for seasonality.

Airport continuity

PBI merged with DJT after the airport was renamed in March 2026. Coverage includes 434 airports.
